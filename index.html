Push notifications when the app is closedtap to open
A free GitHub job checks your deadlines and priorities every 15 minutes and sends a real push through the free ntfy app: a morning summary at 7 AM, a heads-up at 8 PM for tomorrow, and a reminder 1 hour before anything with a time. One-time setup, about 10 minutes. Full steps are in push-setup.md.
1. Install the free ntfy app, tap “+”, and subscribe to your topic (keep the default server ntfy.sh).
2. In your GitHub repo, add a secret named GIST_ID with the Gist ID above.
3. Add remind.mjs to the repo and create .github/workflows/remind.yml.
4. Tap “Send test notification” to check it reaches your phone.
